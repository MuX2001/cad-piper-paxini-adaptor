# Design notes

2026-10-11: Selected a two-piece bolted clamshell over the Piper finger's distal section after comparing it with a friction sleeve and an unverified stock-feature bracket. The split clamp captures the tapered finger, uses a printed distal cap against sliding, and has an inner plate for the supplied M2324 mounting pattern. Four external M3 bolts avoid drilling the stock finger. A removable compliant liner is expected to remove play without marking the original surface. See [design-rationale.md](design-rationale.md).

2026-10-11: The user confirmed two M2324 sensors facing each other. Both reference fingers use equivalent local geometry, checked independently. Each jaw receives the same inner/roof parts using its URDF transform; active normals oppose. Cable windows/lugs are provisional provisions, not validated routes. Each sensor reaches 11.403 mm inward, about 22.805 mm total. V2 supersedes the earlier 25 mm trial floor with a conservative 30 mm software trial floor, not a final limit.

Open design decisions: confirm both physical finger dimensions and coatings, screw head geometry, liner thickness, both cable plugs and bend radii, gripping force, and the permissible command floor. No load test, print test, or physical assembly has been performed.

2026-10-11 v2: Reproduced the baseline with all 59 locked dependencies on the replacement machine. Kept the cavity, inner plate and sensor pose unchanged. Reduced M3 head recess to 0.5 mm and opened its mouth through the sloping outer face; added only an external lug lead-in for distal-end-down printing. See [parameter and screw calculations](m2324-v2-changes.md). Print compensation remains zero. No liner grooves or material choice were introduced; upper/lower thicknesses and compression remain unknown. The [validation report](validation-report.md) records positive rigid clearances and a formula for measured liner interference. The manual's IN/OUT interfaces require real plug, service-loop and bend-space measurements. Full printing is held by [PRINT-HOLD](../PRINT-HOLD.md).
